Antresol for Android TV is the big-screen app for Antresol, a self-hosted home media server. It plays the films, TV shows, music, audiobooks and comics you keep on your own computer or NAS, made for the remote control.

The app needs your own Antresol server (free, runs on Linux, macOS or Docker). No content is provided: you add only media you have the rights to.

Films and TV shows
• A home screen with the newest additions, Continue Watching and every shelf of your library
• Resume where you stopped on the phone or in the browser
• Audio track, subtitle and quality menus; direct play when the TV can decode the file, including Dolby audio passthrough, a converted stream when it can't
• Seasons and episodes, watchlist and watched marks, the next episode after a countdown

Music
• Albums, artists, songs, genres and playlists, Shuffle All
• Keeps playing while you browse, with the system's now-playing card
• Synced lyrics, Up Next, shuffle, repeat, favourites and a sleep timer

Audiobooks
• Chapters, back 15 s / forward 30 s, speeds from 0.75× to 3×, sleep timer
• The position syncs with the server

Comics and manga
• Full-screen pages turned with the remote, right to left for manga

Family
• A profile picker with gradient avatars, password-protected and kid profiles

Privacy
The developer collects no data. The app talks only to the server address you enter; the server runs on your hardware and is controlled by you.

Set up the server: https://sidun-av.github.io/antresol/install.html
