Antresol is the player app for Antresol, a self-hosted home media server. It plays the films, TV shows, music, audiobooks and comics you keep on your own computer or NAS, in one clean library.

The app needs your own Antresol server (free, runs on Linux, macOS or Docker). No content is provided: you add only media you have the rights to.

Films and TV shows
• Pick up where you left off on any device
• Audio tracks, subtitles and quality you choose; direct play when the phone can decode the file, a converted stream when it can't
• Picture in picture, double tap to skip, the next episode after a countdown
• Watchlist and watched marks for films, seasons and episodes

Music
• Albums, artists, songs, genres and playlists; create and edit playlists
• Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls
• Synced lyrics, queue, shuffle, repeat, favourites and a sleep timer

Audiobooks
• Chapters, 15/30 second skips, speeds from 0.75× to 3×, sleep timer
• The position syncs with the server, so you can continue on the TV or in the browser

Comics, manga and PDF
• A reader that turns pages in the right direction for manga, with pinch and double-tap zoom
• The page you stopped at is saved to the server

Family
• Profiles with gradient avatars, passwords and kid profiles with age limits set on the server

Privacy
The developer collects no data. The app talks only to the server address you enter; the server runs on your hardware and is controlled by you.
